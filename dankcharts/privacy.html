2. Scope & How the Service Works

dankcharts.fm is a personal music chart tool. It lets you explore your listening history from one of three data sources:

  • Last.fm — your scrobble history is fetched by your browser from Last.fm's API using your username.
  • Google Sheets — you provide a shared spreadsheet link and your browser reads it from Google. If Google blocks that direct read, the Service may fetch the sheet through our own server (see section 6) and pass it straight back to your browser. Our server does not keep a copy.
  • File upload — you upload a CSV, ZIP or XLSX file (for example a Last.fm, Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube export). It is read in your browser and saved only in your browser's storage on that device.
We don't keep a copy of your full listening history. It is worked out in your browser and cached on your own device. The exceptions are described in section 3: the things you make and save to your account (which name songs, artists and albums), the weekly top 100 you choose to send to The Dank 100, and anything you post publicly.

To show pictures, videos, previews, lyrics, genres, concerts and other extras, your browser (or, for a few lookups, our server) sends song, album and artist names to outside services. These are listed in section 6.

Signing in with Google is optional. Without it, the Service still works, but nothing is saved to an account and nothing follows you to another device.

3.2 Settings and things you make — only when you sign in

If you sign in, the following is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), linked to your account, so it can follow you across devices. Only you can read it.

  • Settings: data source, Last.fm username, Google Sheet link, ID and tab, the display name and time zone you set, theme, language, week start day, chart sizes and display options, auto-correct rules, release-type, album and certification settings, share-picture designs, and similar options.
  • Connection keys you choose to add: your Last.fm scrobbling session (created when you connect Last.fm so you can add or love plays), your own Last.fm API key and secret, your Google Apps Script link for saving plays to your Sheet, and your own Gemini and Ticketmaster API keys. These are saved so the features keep working on your other devices. Anyone holding them could act on those services as you, so we keep them in your private account area only. You can remove them in Settings at any time.
  • Your work: award picks for each year, song and album ratings, playlists, smart playlists and automatic chart playlists, your listening diary, genre edits, picture choices, saved comparisons, game progress, guide progress, recent searches, notifications, a cache of concert lookups, and the TV remote link between your devices (what is playing and the buttons you press).
  • Backups: copies of your settings, awards and ratings, made automatically and when you ask, so a mistake can be undone from Settings → Profile. Up to 30 of each kind are kept; older ones are removed automatically.

Because these items name songs, artists and albums, they say something about your music taste. They are used only to run the features you use.

3.3 The Dank 100 entries — only when you send one

The Dank 100 is an optional shared weekly chart. If you are signed in and press Send my week (or switch on Send my week automatically in Settings → Charts), the following is stored in Firebase, linked to your account, for that week:

  • Your top 100 songs, artists and albums for the week (titles, artist names and how many times you played each)
  • Your total number of plays that week
  • When the entry was sent

Only you can read your own entry. Everyone's entries are added up on our server into one combined chart, and only that combined chart is public. It never shows who sent what. If very few people sent in a week, the combined chart can still look a lot like one person's chart. To keep the chart fair, our server compares entries to spot duplicate or fake accounts, and we may leave an account out of the count. You can take an entry back while its week is open, and ask us to delete older ones (see section 8).

3.4 Things you choose to make public

  • Board posts and replies: anything you post on the Board — the title, text, pictures you attach, any smart playlist you attach, votes, and the time — is public. Anyone can read it, with or without an account. It is shown with your display name and Google profile photo.
  • Shared awards ceremonies: when you create a ceremony share link, a copy of that year's ceremony (the categories, nominees, winners and any names, play counts and pictures shown in it) is saved so that anyone with the link can watch it. The link is hard to guess but is not secret once you send it to someone. You can update or delete it at any time.

Please don't post anything you want to keep private. Other people can copy or screenshot what they see.

3.5 Support messages

When you use Contact Support, we receive your email address and message, plus the page you were on, your interface language, your browser type, and — if you are signed in — your display name, account email and account ID. This is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms (see section 6) and used only to answer you and fix problems.

3.6 Welcome email

The first time you sign in, your name and email address are sent to our server, which sends you one welcome email through our email provider, Resend. We do not send marketing emails or newsletters.

3.7 Shared video cache

When the player finds a YouTube video for a song, the song search (stored as a scrambled code) and the video ID are saved in a shared cache so the next person doesn't have to search again. This cache does not record who made the search.

3.8 Analytics data

The Service uses GoatCounter for page-view analytics. GoatCounter:

  • Sets no cookies
  • Does not store persistent identifiers or track you across sites
  • Records only overall counts (page views, referring site, browser, language, screen size, country)

Overall traffic counts are publicly visible at erwindank.goatcounter.com. They do not identify you.

3.9 Technical data

Like any website, the companies that host the Service (Cloudflare, Google Firebase and Render) receive your IP address, browser type and the time of each request, and may keep short-lived logs for security and to keep the Service running. We do not use this data to identify or track you.

3.10 Data we do NOT collect

  • Your Google, Last.fm, Spotify, Apple Music or other passwords
  • Payment information (the Service is currently free; if paid features are introduced, payments will be handled by a payment provider and this policy will be updated first)

8. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Account, settings, your work: kept until you delete it or ask us to delete your account.
  • Backups: up to 30 of each kind; older ones are removed automatically. All are deleted with your account.
  • The Dank 100 entries: kept with your account so the site can show which rows you helped chart. The combined weekly charts contain no personal data and are kept indefinitely.
  • Board posts, replies and pictures: kept until you or a moderator delete them, or until your account is deleted.
  • Shared ceremonies: kept until you delete the link, or until your account is deleted.
  • Support messages: kept in our inbox for as long as needed to help you and keep a record, and deleted on request.
  • Hosting logs: kept for short periods set by each hosting provider.
  • GoatCounter counts and the shared video cache: contain no personal data and may be kept indefinitely.

To delete your account and data:

  1. Email yourdata@dankcharts.fm from your account's email address with the subject line "Delete my account".
  2. Within 30 days we will permanently delete your sign-in record and everything stored under your account (section 3.2), your Dank 100 entries, your Board posts, replies and pictures, and your shared ceremony links. Copies in providers' own backups are removed on their normal schedule.

Data kept in your own browser (your cached listening history, uploaded files and local settings) is not on our servers. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

11. Your Rights — EU & UK (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have the following rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — ask us to delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restriction of processing — ask us to limit how we process your data.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (e.g., JSON).
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (such as The Dank 100), you may withdraw at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at yourdata@dankcharts.fm. We may need to confirm the request comes from the account owner. We will respond within one month; for complex requests we may extend this by a further two months and will tell you if so.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g., your national DPA within the EU, or the ICO in the UK).

12. Your Rights — California (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the CPRA, gives you the following rights:

  • Right to know — the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you. The categories are: identifiers (name, email, account ID), internet activity (request data), and the content you create (settings, ratings, awards, playlists, posts).
  • Right to delete — request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to correct — ask us to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing — we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to non-discrimination — we will not treat you differently for exercising any of your rights.

To exercise these rights, email yourdata@dankcharts.fm. We will respond within 45 days, with the option to extend by a further 45 days when reasonably necessary.
