2. About the Service

dankcharts.fm is a personal music chart tool. It builds charts, stats, records, awards and playlists from your listening history, which you connect from:

  • Last.fm — your scrobble history, fetched from Last.fm's API using your username.
  • Google Sheets — a shared spreadsheet you provide.
  • File upload — a CSV, ZIP or XLSX export you upload in your browser.

Optional extras include signing in with Google to sync across devices, adding plays to Last.fm or your Sheet, the song player, The Dank 100 shared chart, the community Board, shared awards ceremonies, a TV remote, and lookups for artwork, lyrics, genres, concerts and world charts.
